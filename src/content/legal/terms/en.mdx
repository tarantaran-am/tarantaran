## What Taran Taran is

Taran Taran is a directory of wedding vendors in Armenia: venues, photographers, decorators and more. The site helps you find a vendor and contact them directly. We do not provide wedding services and do not take payment for them.

## Your agreement with a vendor

We are not a party to any agreement between you and a vendor. Timing, quality of service and payment are agreed directly between you and the vendor, and the vendor is responsible for them.

## Information in listings

Descriptions, photos and contact details are provided by the vendors themselves. We review each listing before publishing it, but we do not guarantee that the information is complete or up to date when you get in touch.

## Ranking

Listing in the directory is free. Vendors who pay for promotion appear higher in their category and on the homepage, and are marked with a “Top” badge.

## For vendors

- We create your listing after you contact us on WhatsApp.
- You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you provide and for the rights to your photos.
- To change or remove your listing, write to us and we will do it within a reasonable time.
- We may decline or remove a listing if the information is inaccurate or we receive well-founded complaints about the vendor.
