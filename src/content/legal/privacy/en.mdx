This policy explains what data [tarantaran.am](https://tarantaran.am) collects, why, who it is shared with and how long it is kept. Data is processed in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Armenia on Protection of Personal Data.

## Who processes your data

The data controller is the owner of the Taran Taran website, a private individual. For any questions about your data, write to [tarantaran.am@gmail.com](mailto:tarantaran.am@gmail.com).

## Requests to vendors

When you send a request through the form on a vendor's page, we receive:

- your name and phone number;
- the event date and your message, if you provided them;
- your preferred way to be contacted — WhatsApp or Telegram, if you ticked it;
- the language you were using the site in.

We use this data for one purpose: to deliver your request to the vendor you chose so they can get in touch with you. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by submitting the form.

Once sent, the request goes to the vendor you chose. From that point on, the vendor processes your data independently and is responsible for doing so. We do not use requests for advertising or mailing lists, and we never sell them.

## Vendor page statistics

We count how often visitors open a vendor's contacts and follow their links, so we can show vendors how useful their listing is. For this we store the action, its date and the site language. So that one visitor is not counted twice in a day, we also store a hash — a value derived one-way from the IP address and browser details that changes every day. It cannot be used to recover the IP address or identify the visitor.

## Spam protection

To limit the number of requests sent from one address, a hash of the IP address is stored with each request. The IP address itself is not stored.

## Technical data

- **Errors and performance.** If an error occurs on the site, details about it — the page address, browser and device type, technical information — are sent to the Sentry error-monitoring service. For some visits, Sentry also measures how fast pages load.
- **Hosting logs.** Our hosting provider, Vercel, keeps standard logs of requests to the site.

## Cookies and browser storage

- The `NEXT_LOCALE` cookie remembers your chosen language.
- After you send a request, your name and phone number are saved in your browser's storage so you don't have to type them again next time. This data stays on your device only; to delete it, clear the site data in your browser settings.

The site uses no advertising or analytics cookies.

## Who we share data with

- **The vendor** your request is addressed to, as described above.
- **Providers that keep the site running:** Supabase (database), Vercel (hosting), Sentry (error monitoring) and Telegram (service notifications). They process data only to provide their services. The database and hosting servers are located in the European Union (Germany), so data is transferred outside Armenia.
- **Public authorities**, only where the law expressly requires it.

## How long we keep data

- Requests are kept for 12 months from the date they are sent and then deleted.
- Vendor page statistics cannot identify anyone and are kept indefinitely.
- Technical data is kept for the periods set by the respective service providers.

## Your rights

You can:

- find out what data we hold about you;
- ask us to correct or delete it;
- withdraw your consent to processing.

Write to [tarantaran.am@gmail.com](mailto:tarantaran.am@gmail.com) and we will reply within the time limits set by the laws of the Republic of Armenia. We cannot delete a request from a vendor once it has been delivered — for that, please contact the vendor directly. If you believe your rights have been violated, you may contact the authorised personal data protection body of the Republic of Armenia.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page, with the date of the latest update at the top.
