## What data we collect

The site does not collect or store your personal data. There is no sign-up, and whatever you type into the form on a vendor page is not sent to us or to the vendor.

When you call a vendor or message them, you share your details with them directly, and from then on they handle that data.

## Cookies

The site uses one technical cookie, `NEXT_LOCALE`, which remembers your language. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

## Technical data

If an error occurs on the site, details about it — the page address and your browser and device type — are sent to the Sentry error monitoring service so that we can fix it. Our hosting provider keeps standard request logs.
