## A wedding DJ

The DJ takes over when the toasts give way to dancing. At an Armenian wedding the dance floor is full all evening, so the DJ needs to blend Armenian music, from kochari to modern pop, with Russian and international tracks, and work together with the tamada and live musicians.

## How to choose a DJ

- ask whether they have played large Armenian weddings and how they build a playlist;
- check whether they bring their own speakers, microphones for toasts and lighting;
- discuss how they work with the tamada and musicians so there are no gaps;
- agree on a do-not-play list.

## What to prepare

Make a list of must-play tracks: the couple's entrance, the first dance, the dance with the parents, the cake. Check the sound in the hall with the DJ in advance: many venues have their own system, and it may not be powerful enough for a large hall.
