## Wedding cars: the cortege and the couple's car

The wedding cortege is a visible part of an Armenian wedding: the cars take the groom to collect the bride, then on to the church and the banquet. The couple usually take one executive car or a convertible, with several matching cars for family and guests.

## How to choose

- check whether the car comes with a driver and whether that is included;
- ask about the minimum rental time and how overtime is charged;
- ask for photos of the exact car you will get;
- agree on decoration: ribbons and flowers from the rental company or your decorator.

## Planning the route

Map out the route in advance: the groom's home, the bride's home, the church, the photo shoot location, the venue. Leave time for traffic, since Yerevan is busy on Friday and Saturday evenings and a photo shoot at Garni or Lake Sevan adds hours on the road.
