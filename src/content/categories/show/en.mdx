## Wedding entertainment

Entertainment marks the evening's key moments: the couple's entrance, the first dance, the cake, the finale. At Armenian weddings the most common choices are dance acts, including national dances, dhol and zurna, light and fire shows, cold spark fountains and confetti.

## How to choose an act

The show has to suit the venue and the guests. Ask the venue whether fire and pyrotechnics are allowed indoors, how high the ceilings are and where the stage will be. Ask the performers for a full video of a performance and find out how long the act lasts and how much space it needs.

## What to agree on

- timing: when in the evening they perform and for how long;
- technical needs: sound, light, power, a dressing room;
- who announces the act: the tamada, the host or the planner;
- safety: fire and pyrotechnics need the venue's permission and distance from guests.
