## Wedding catering and buffets

Catering is needed when the wedding isn't in a restaurant: at a countryside venue, in a garden, in a marquee or at home. Another common case is a buffet after the church ceremony or a welcome area for guests while the couple are at their photo shoot.

## Banquet, buffet or a mix

An Armenian wedding is traditionally built around a generous feast, so weddings away from restaurants usually order a full banquet with service. A buffet suits an intimate wedding, a cocktail hour or a reception after the ceremony.

## What to ask a caterer

- the menu and quantities per guest, and whether there is a tasting;
- what is included: tableware, linen, furniture, waiters, a bartender;
- whether the site needs a kitchen, water and power;
- how delivery, setup and clearing are handled;
- whether you can bring your own alcohol and if there is a corkage fee.
