## A wedding dress: buy, make or rent

There are three ways to a wedding dress. A ready-made dress from a salon can be tried on straight away, but almost always needs alterations. Having one made gives full control over the silhouette and fabric and suits non-standard sizes. Renting saves money, usually costing a quarter to a third of buying a similar dress.

## Timing

- ready-made: start choosing five to six months ahead, allowing for alterations;
- made to order: find a dressmaker four to five months ahead to get through the fittings calmly;
- rental: look at dresses two to three months ahead and book one to two months ahead.

If the wedding is in May, June or September, add a month to each: salons and dressmakers are busy in season.

## What to keep in mind

The dress stays with the bride all day, from the church ceremony to the dancing. The church calls for covered shoulders or a wrap, the banquet for freedom of movement. Many brides in Armenia choose a second, lighter outfit for the evening.
