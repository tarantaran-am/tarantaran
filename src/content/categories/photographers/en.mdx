## How to choose a wedding photographer

The photographer is the one vendor whose work stays with you for decades. Look beyond the highlights and ask for one or two full weddings: that shows how the photographer handles difficult light in a church, the banquet and the dancing.

## Documentary or posed

Most wedding photographers in Armenia work somewhere between two poles. Documentary photography catches real emotion: the church ceremony, the elders' toasts, the shurjpar circle dance. Posed photography gives polished portraits of the couple. At a big Armenian wedding with a packed schedule, purely posed shooting can't keep up, so a documentary eye is valued.

## What to agree on before booking

- when the shoot starts: preparations, the bride's ransom or straight at the church;
- how long the photographer stays at the banquet;
- whether you need a second photographer, since one person can't be everywhere at a wedding of 250–400 guests;
- the deadline and format of the photos, and whether there is a photo book;
- how travel is charged if the portraits or banquet are outside Yerevan.
