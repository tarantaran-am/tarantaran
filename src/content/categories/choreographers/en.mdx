## Wedding dance choreography

The couple's first dance is one of the most moving moments of the wedding, and everyone films it: the photographer, the videographer and the guests. A choreographer sets the dance to your music and your level, even if you have never danced.

## How many lessons you need

A simple waltz or slow dance usually takes 5–8 lessons. A dance with lifts or changes of style takes 10–15. Start two to three months before the wedding so you are not rehearsing in the final week.

## What else choreographers stage

- the father–daughter or mother–son dance;
- a number with the bridesmaids and groomsmen;
- the couple's entrance into the hall;
- Armenian dances such as kochari or shurjpar for the couple and guests.

Rehearse in the shoes you will wear on the day, and at least once in the dress. Above are wedding dance choreographers in Yerevan and the regions.
