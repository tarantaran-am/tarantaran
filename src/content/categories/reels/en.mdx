## A wedding reels maker: what it is and why

A reels maker films the wedding on a phone and edits short vertical videos for Instagram and TikTok, on the day itself or right after. It doesn't replace a videographer: the videographer makes a film to keep, the reels maker makes quick, lively videos you can share the same evening.

## What the work includes

A reels maker usually films the preparations, the bride's entrance, the ceremony, the first dance and the banquet's best moments, then hands over several finished reels and the raw footage. Some post the first reel while the party is still going.

## How to choose a reels maker

- look through their account: editing pace, music, colour;
- ask how many reels you will get and when;
- agree on the moments to capture and what not to post;
- let the photographer and videographer know there will be a reels maker so they don't get in each other's way.
