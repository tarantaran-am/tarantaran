## Bridal hair and makeup

The bride's look has to last through a long Armenian wedding day: getting ready, the ransom, the church ceremony, the photo shoot and a banquet that runs late. So a bridal makeup artist and hairstylist are expected to deliver not just beauty but staying power: through hugs, tears in church and dancing.

## The trial

The trial is the most important thing to do in advance. Bring photos of the dress, jewellery and veil, show your references, and take pictures in daylight and with a flash. That shows how the look will read on camera.

## What to ask the stylist

- travel to your home or hotel, and when to start;
- how many people are on the team if your mother, sisters and friends need doing too;
- whether the stylist stays for touch-ups or a change of look for the banquet;
- which products they use: how long they last and whether they are hypoallergenic.
