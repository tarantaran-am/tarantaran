## How to choose a wedding venue

The banquet hall is the biggest item in a wedding budget and the first thing couples book. The venue's free dates set the wedding date, and its size and location decide the guest count, the logistics and half of the other vendors: decor, sound, the photographer.

At an Armenian wedding whole families are invited, and 250–400 guests is normal. So start with the guest list rather than pretty photos: first the number, then the halls that really fit it together with a dance floor and a stage.

## Restaurant, banquet hall or countryside venue

In Yerevan, weddings are held in banquet halls with their own kitchen, in restaurants with a private hall and at countryside venues with a garden in Kotayk, by Lake Sevan or in Tsaghkadzor. A banquet hall is the simplest: menu, service and space in one contract. A countryside venue gives room and an open-air ceremony, but adds guest transport and depends on the weather.

## What to compare

- capacity with the dance floor and stage taken into account;
- what the price per guest includes and what is paid separately;
- rules on your own alcohol, cake and outside vendors;
- parking and the drive from the church where the ceremony takes place;
- how late you can celebrate and what an extension costs.
