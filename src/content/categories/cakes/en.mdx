## A custom wedding cake

The cake is one of the most photographed moments of the banquet. At an Armenian wedding it is brought out with a show, to music and sparkler fountains, so taste, looks and size all matter: there has to be enough for a large number of guests.

## How to choose a pastry chef

Ask for a tasting of two or three fillings and look at real photos of cakes from past weddings, not just sketches. Ask how many tiers your guest count needs and how the cake will handle the drive and a warm hall in summer, since cream finishes behave differently.

## What to discuss

- the cake's weight per guest;
- decoration: fresh flowers, sugar flowers, toppers;
- delivery and assembly at the venue;
- the venue's rules, as some charge for an outside cake;
- whether you want a dessert table as well as the cake.
