## Wedding video: clip, film or full coverage

A videographer captures what photos can't: voices, toasts, music, the movement of a dance. A wedding day usually becomes a short 3–5 minute clip, a 15–30 minute film and, if you like, full unedited coverage of the ceremony and toasts.

## How to choose a videographer

Ask to watch one wedding film in full, not just the showreel. Pay attention to sound: can you hear the vows in the church and the toasts at the table, how clean is the voice recording. Ask whether the videographer works with an assistant and a drone, and how they coordinate with the photographer so they stay out of each other's shots.

## What to agree on in advance

- which parts of the day are filmed and what you receive;
- editing time, which grows in high season;
- whether you can choose the music and how many revisions are included;
- whether you need a separate short video for social media, which a reels maker often handles.
