## Wedding decor: the hall, the ceremony and the flowers

Decor turns a banquet hall into the space of your wedding: the couple's table, table settings, a ceremony arch, a photo zone, lighting and flowers. The decorator is usually chosen after the venue, because the design is always built around a specific space.

## Where to start

Gather 10–15 references you like and settle on a palette. A good decorator will propose a concept and a sketch, and point out what the hall already has and what is better hidden. Fresh flowers are the most expensive part, so budgets are often balanced with a mix of flowers, greenery and fabric.

## What to discuss with the decorator

- what the quote includes: item rental, flowers, setup and teardown;
- when the team can get into the hall and how long setup takes;
- church decor and the bridal bouquet, separately or in the same order;
- what happens to the flowers after the wedding.
